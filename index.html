04 ( about )

I like making complicated things a little simpler.

I specialize in pathogen detection — working with genomic data where accuracy and scale genuinely matter. Somewhere along the way I fell for the web and for programming: the craft of taking a messy, complicated problem and shaping it into something clean that simply works.

What I enjoy most is finding the simple solution — the one that actually helps people solve a real problem or move their project forward. A genomics pipeline or a polished web app, it's the same goal every time: build something genuinely useful, without the friction.
